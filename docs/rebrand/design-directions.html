A recommendation, since you asked for opinions

Workbench fits the positioning best: the pitch is "measured, instrumented, no theater" — and the aesthetic literally is that. It keeps brand continuity with the orange, reads distinctly against every competitor in the market (who are all shipping soft SaaS gradients), and its annotation vocabulary gives service pages and pricing tables a natural structure.

Editorial is the right call if the blog is meant to become the firm's front door. Signal is the boldest, but spends its boldness on volume rather than substance — riskier for a firm selling judgment.
